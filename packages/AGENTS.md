# packages

Shared code. Packages never import an app or a module. Each exposes its TypeScript source from `index.ts` (`main`, or `exports` when it has subpaths), extends `@domus/tsconfig/base.json` and has a `type-check` script; only the apps build. A package with tests adds `test` and a `vitest.config.ts`.

- `platform`: the door (`http`: Better Auth's sign-in and sign-out, the platform's operations such as `password/change`, `sessions/list`, `confirmations/confirm` and `/reject`, `proofs/request`, `delegation/read` (a read on the path of the actions, like `log/read`), and `log/read` and `log/undo` (one's own entries only), and the sensitive writes `permissions/set`, `delegation/set`, `sessions/revoke` and `devices/revoke`, the scheduled tasks' `tasks/list` (a read) and the `manage` writes `tasks/setTime`, `tasks/pause`, `tasks/resume` (undoable) and `tasks/launch` (conflict `task-busy` with a run pending or in progress), `notifications/test` (the test notice, an irreversible write of a person by itself with no level of any area and no proof, `personal` of authorization), and each module action at `<module>/<action>`, all but `status` only with a session; every answer under `/api/*`, errors and Better Auth's included, carries `Cache-Control: no-store`; besides oRPC, the Hono routes `POST /api/files` (upload) and `POST /api/files/download`, and `GET /api/notifications/key`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/notifications/subscription`, only with a session, answering errors as `ORPCError`'s JSON), `files` (ADR 0028: the store under the `files` folder of the data folder, `contents/<2 chars>/<sha256>` and `incoming/`, contents never rewritten and published under an in-process lock per hash; an upload (type by its bytes, checked name, at most 25 MB, reservations per session of 20 files or 200 MB and 2 GB left free, a minute without bytes ends it) gives a prepared file of its session, single use, for 24 h; `references.ts` is `ctx.files`, whose `detach` in an undoable write releases the reference linked to its entry and only that entry's undo may `reattach` it; a download runs a read on the path of the actions and opens a file it returned marked `fileRef()`, and a confirmed one only through its route; the cleanup's sweeps of the store; `needed.ts`, the contents a backup carries and a restore checks; module-sdk exposes only `ctx.files` and a dependency rule keeps modules out), `identity` (Better Auth, devices, each with the `entry` a session entering it writes before it is created and the session's matching `deviceEntry` that makes it the one the device has enabled for notices (`sessionEnabledWhere`), roles, permission rows, each account's delegation table and reading it, passwords and sessions, with the one rule that a session lives in `sessions.ts`: its row exists, is the account's and has not expired on the platform clock, and the core of `pnpm accounts`: `parseAccountsArguments` and `runAccounts`), `actions` (the one path of every action: `invoke` and `undo`, with a verified context; when authorization asks for confirmation the operation waits in `platform_confirmations` and answers `CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED`, and `confirm` and `reject` answer it; a sensitive operation, level `manage` or one of the platform's own in `identity/operations.ts`, needs a step-up proof of that exact operation, kept hashed in `platform_proofs` and used in its transaction, else `STEP_UP_REQUIRED`), `authorization` (the only decision: a pure `decide` over roles, permission rows, delegation and areas, ADR 0023), `audit` (the action log over `platform/0001`), `background` (ADR 0025: the engine of jobs in `platform_jobs`, in two lanes, short and maintenance, one at a time each and both at once; a maximum time per job kind aborts its signal, and one that goes on 10 s more is stuck, its lane taken until it ends; a `PermanentJobError` fails at once, keeping its code and message, any other error is retried from 30 s doubling up to 5 attempts, keeping only its name and code, and a `PassingJobError` with `notBefore` not before that instant; a job's work receives its id and `retryAt`, the instant a passing error of that attempt would retry at, and may end with an `outcome`, kept in its row; besides its tasks' kinds, the platform's own (`kinds`, the notices'); the outbox (ADR 0029): a job kind with data declares its schema, checked when a job is claimed (`INVALID_PAYLOAD`, a failure for good), and a write leaves a job in its transaction, after its log entry and with its number in `entry`, waking only after the commit, and the undo of that entry cancels its pending jobs with the reason `undone`, claiming and cancelling both checking `pending`; a job kind's `failed`, when it has one, runs in the transaction of a failure for good (not of a cancellation); `start` recovers what a previous process left running or stuck, and `stop` waits 5 s at most; the scheduler of the platform's tasks at a local time of `Europe/Madrid`, each planned run unique by task and local date in `platform_task_runs`; the daily `cleanup`, in batches of its own transaction, which empties the content of expired log entries and the operation of expired confirmations, deletes the proofs that decide nothing and old jobs (30 days) and runs (90 days), and never changes an answer; the settings of the tasks as platform operations in `background/operations.ts`, whose changes wake it only after the action's transaction; the watcher, `health()`, which the door adds to `/health` as `background`: `status` `ok` or `degraded`, `reasons` only when degraded, every one at once in a fixed order (`scheduler-silent`: no wake for more than 5 min, it wakes at least every minute; `lane-stuck`; then the backups' `backup-stale`, `backup-retention-failed`, `backup-check-failed`), and `backups` always (`off`, `none-yet`, `none` once an attempt failed with none saved, `saved`); it waits only through the platform's clock and timer; `apps/api` starts it with `startBackground` and stops it with `stopBackground` before `close`, which throws while it is active; module-sdk exposes none of it and a dependency rule keeps modules out), `backups` (ADR 0026, ADR 0027: on only with `backups` among the options of opening, a repository and a password file; the daily `backup` at 05:00 in the maintenance lane empties the staging folder `backup-staging` of the data folder, notes the capture instant before the copy starts, has a child Node process with its own connection take the shared lock (`TAKE_SHARED_LOCK` of `lock.ts`) and `VACUUM INTO` there as `domus.db` (a copy the child does not end well fails at once), runs `quick_check` in another child, which also lists from the copy the file contents it needs at the capture (`files/needed.ts`: live references and released ones still undoable then), hard-links each into the staging folder under `files/` as the store lays them out (one missing fails at once) and saves the copy and those links with restic (`--files-from-verbatim`, at `/domus.db` and `/files/contents/…`), a child process with `--no-cache`, host `domus`, the capture as its time, and the repository and password only in its environment, after `unlock`, initializing only an empty destination; then `forget --prune`, 7 daily, 4 weekly, 3 monthly, grouped by host, waiting up to 10 min for a lock (`--retry-lock 10m`), so only a lock still held after that fails the retention; restic's code 3 (partial) and any but 11 (locked, passing) fail at once with the step and the code, and a failed retention fails the run without a retry, the backup saved; the weekly `backup-check`, Sunday at 05:30 in the maintenance lane, runs `unlock` and `check --read-data`, ends well without touching its mark while the repository does not exist, retries a live lock and marks the check failed on any failure for good, used-up retries included; a timed-out or stopped job sends its child, the snapshot's included, SIGTERM and, 5 s later on the platform timer, SIGKILL, settling only once the child ended; the one row of `platform_backup_marks`, loaded at opening and kept in memory, feeds the watcher; restoring, `backups/restore.ts` with `restore.mjs`'s core in `backups/command.ts`, checks the modules as opening does, holds the exclusive lock, stages the snapshot and its file contents in `restore-staging` (restic, `quick_check`, the migrator's check and missing migrations, the SHA-256 of every content the copy needs (`files/needed.ts`), one missing or wrong refusing, then in one transaction jobs pending, running or stuck cancelled `discarded by restore` with their runs, next runs from now, every session, pending confirmation and proof ended, every prepared file, released reference past its undo and content row nothing points to dropped, the provenance and a newer saved mark), closes it in `DELETE` mode, renames every needed content over the live store's, synced (never deleting one; what a cut leaves is for the cleanup), and installs it: `before-restore-<instant>` of hard links, the marker `restore.pending` published atomically, the old `-wal` and `-shm` removed, the rename, the marker removed, the staging folder emptied, every step synced; a marker found resumes from removing the auxiliaries, and opening refuses with `restore incomplete: run restore again` or `restore marker unreadable`; module-sdk exposes none of it and a dependency rule keeps modules out), `lock.ts` (ADR 0026: the lock of the database, an SQLite file `domus.lock` in `DELETE` mode beside `domus.db`, never replaced nor deleted; opening on a file holds it shared, a real read taken before touching the data and freed by `close` or a failed open; restoring takes it exclusive without waiting, else `the database is in use: stop api and accounts first`; opening while it is exclusive fails with `restore in progress`; in memory there is none; inside a process only SQLite may open that file, since closing any other descriptor of it drops the process's `fcntl` locks; module-sdk exposes none of it and the backups' dependency rule keeps modules out), `schema.ts` (its `platform_` tables in Drizzle; `pnpm db:generate` writes its migrations from `platform/0002` on, after a base that stands for the hand-written `0000` + `0001` and never runs, ADR 0023), `events` (ADR 0029: the live channel `GET /api/events`, a route of Hono with `streamSSE` served through the door, only with a session that lives on the platform clock; a write leaves its changed records (`changesOf` of the capture: first before, last after) in a temporary table of its transaction, shared out once no transaction is open, with the records of area tables it declares touched, in commit order, one `invalidate` per write to each channel that `authorization/invalidations.ts` lets see a record (in an area table, `read` in its area); `ready` once registered; a heartbeat every 25 s of the platform timer, and each sharing, check the session and end it with `purge`; ending sessions (`endSessions` of `identity/sessions.ts`, the one path, and Better Auth's hook on signing out) and `permissions/set` leave their mark in the same table, in their transaction: `purge` and closing on the ended sessions' channels, `reset` on the account's, closing after it when it lost `read` in the area (`readLost`); what ends sessions outside `api` the heartbeat finds; 6 channels per session, the seventh cuts the oldest; a queue of 100 unwritten events, past it the channel is cut without `purge`; module-sdk exposes none of it and a dependency rule keeps modules out), `notifications` (ADR 0029: the VAPID keys, one row of `platform_push_keys` created the first time the platform opens, the public one served; a subscription per device and per endpoint in `platform_push_subscriptions`, hanging from its session and device, registered by a person by itself (`maySubscribe` of authorization) from the session its device has enabled, checked in the transaction that writes it (`session-not-enabled`), only on an endpoint of the push services admitted (`endpointAllowed`, the runtime's `pushServices`, production's three hosts unless a test gives its own), the same one again changing nothing and anything else a new id; a new session on the device deletes its subscription of another session; `ctx.notify` leaves a `notice` job in its write with `{ to, module, type, id }`, and the test notice (`operations.ts`) one with `{ kind: "test", to }`, whose sharing out and delivery check no record (`testNoticeDecision`: only that `to` is a person) and whose content is `{ kind: "test", notice }`, whose work (`notices.ts`) shares it out in one transaction: its entry not undone, `to` a person who sees the record now (`noticeDecision` of `authorization/notices.ts`, over the facts `records.ts` reads), then one `delivery` job and row of `platform_deliveries` per enabled subscription of `to`, unique per notice and subscription, its deadline the notice's creation plus 24 h, each skip an `outcome`; each delivery checks again, in every attempt, the subscription, its session living and enabled, the record and the endpoint, ending well with its `outcome` when it may not go, and sends what `web-push`'s `generateRequestDetails` encrypts and signs (content `{ module, type, id, notice }`, `TTL: 86400`, `Urgency: normal`) through `fetch` with `redirect: "manual"` and the job's signal, and acts on the answer as `answers.ts` classifies it (2xx `sent`; 404 and 410 forget the subscription, `subscription-gone`; for good `PUSH_REJECTED`, `PUSH_UNAUTHORIZED`, `PUSH_REDIRECT`; 429, 5xx, a network error and a timeout retried, not before a `Retry-After` of 429 and 503), failing with `DELIVERY_EXPIRED` once it is past its deadline or its next attempt would be (`JobContext.retryAt`); the subject of VAPID is the first `https` one of the platform's `origins`, else `PUSH_NOT_CONFIGURED`; module-sdk exposes only `ctx.notify` and a dependency rule keeps modules out). How actions validate, authorize, log, repeat, undo and expire: ADR 0022; a repeat answers an acknowledgement without content (ADR 0023). `@domus/platform/testing` holds what only `test-utils` may import and no production code reaches: a module's raw Drizzle client, a platform with a given clock, synthetic verified contexts, the privileged preparation of accounts, permission and delegation rows and sessions, the real request of a step-up proof, the real read of a delegation table, and the action log, the stored pending confirmations and the log of task runs as read-only data, and the background's controlled timer, extra test tasks, synthetic job kinds of either lane and their jobs as data, a synthetic job left in the write in progress, wake and settle, restoring with a given clock, restic and the cut points of its exchange (only here), the listing of snapshots and the backups' marks as data, `fakeNode`, a Node executable for the backups' children that stops the snapshot's child at its copy, the lock held, or spoils the copy, and `startChild`, a child process run through tsx that holds the platform open, the exclusive lock, a restore stopped at a cut point (with the migrations of the modules it is given), an upload stopped once its file is published and before its rows, or a cleanup stopped once it claimed a content or removed its file, or the background stopped once a notice committed its deliveries (`distribute`, with the modules by the URL of their definition), over a data folder, tells the test once it holds it and waits for SIGKILL (ADR 0021, ADR 0022, ADR 0023, ADR 0025, ADR 0026). Another authorizer is only for platform's own tests.
- `platform/contracts` (`@domus/platform/contracts`): the platform's own contracts, such as `status` and `PROTOCOL`. Pure: no Node, no other part of platform. The only part of platform that `web`, `assistant` and `voice-gateway` may import.
- `module-sdk`: the only part of platform a module may use: `defineModule` (areas, tables, migrations, `contract` and `actions`) and `checkModules`, and for the platform `recordTablesOf`, a module's owned and area tables with their name, record type and area; `@domus/module-sdk/db` re-exports what a module needs of Drizzle and adds `moduleTables(id, areas?)`, with `table`, `ownedTable` (owner, visibility and `id` as only key, ADR 0023) and `areaTable` (`id` as only key, the household's records of an area, seen by whoever reads it, ADR 0029), and `id()` (ADR 0021); `@domus/module-sdk/contracts`, pure, offers `readAction({ area?, level, alwaysConfirm? })` and `writeAction({ undoable, area?, level, alwaysConfirm? })` (ADR 0022, ADR 0023), and `fileRef()`, `isFileRef(schema)` and the type `FileRef`, which mark a file in a read's output (ADR 0028). A module gets its database only from a handler's context, with `visible(table)`, `ownership(table)` and, for the referential block of ADR 0024, `editable(table)` and `forbidden()` for its owned tables, built in `platform/authorization` (`editable` from the same rule as the write capture), and `files` (`ctx.files`: `of`, `attach`, `detach` and `reattach` on a `RecordOf`, a record of its own owned tables; changing files from a read or on another table is the module's mistake), and `notify` (`ctx.notify({ to, about: { type, id } })`, a notice in the write about a record of its own owned or area tables; from a read or about another type, the module's mistake). Every write to an owned table passes the write capture (`platform/actions/capture.ts`, its rules in `platform/authorization/writes.ts`): temporary triggers, created after the migrations, note each row transition, checked before commit.
- `ui`: the design system, Vue components (phase 2).
- `i18n`: translation runtime and catalogs.
- `utils`: one business-free topic per folder, imported as `@domus/utils/<topic>`. A topic enters when it knows nothing of the business and two places use it; a topic imports another only through a named exception in `.dependency-cruiser.cjs`. Topics: `connection` (connecting, ready, incompatible, with a 10 s limit per check); `program` (port and address reading, the `/health` server, shutdown on signals); `date-time` (`date-fns` with `@date-fns/tz`: the next daily or weekly occurrence of a local `HH:MM` in a given zone, with the rules of the change of the clocks, and the local date of an instant).
- `test-utils`: what tests use instead of real infrastructure: `createTestPlatform`, a real platform in memory or on a file with the door in process (the only path the rules give the tests of `assistant` and `voice-gateway` to it), with the real authorization, a controllable clock, the privileged `prepare` (accounts, passwords, grants, delegation, sessions), `delegation()`, `requestProof`, `setPermission`, `setDelegation`, `revokeSession`, `revokeDevice`, `testNotice`, `hasSession`, `sessionExpiresAt` (a session's expiry, from its row), `subscriptions()` (each subscription to notices with whether it is enabled, as data), synthetic contexts (`context`, `member`), a cookie-keeping `browser()` for HTTP tests, typed `invoke` and `undo`, `confirm`, `reject` and `pendingFrom` for pending confirmations, `readLog` (the real read of one's annotations), `log()` and `pendingConfirmations()`, and `background` (the same engine and scheduler on the test clock: `start`, `stop`, `runDue`, `settled`, `runs()`, `tasks()`, `pause`, `resume`, `setTime`, `enqueue` (with data), `enqueueInWrite` (from a handler the test wraps), `flush`, `jobs()`; moving the clock wakes what falls due, unless moved with `{ wake: false }`, a silent scheduler; option `tasks` declares test tasks and `jobs` synthetic jobs the test ends with its own promises), and option `backups` on a file (a temporary repository and password file and, only here, the paths of the restic and Node executables), with `background.marks()`, and `restoreBackup` and `listBackups` over the same file and repository, with `fakeNode`, `requireRestic` and `startChild` for the modules' backup and cut tests, and for files option `files` (a harness temporary folder unless the test gives one; `test.filesDirectory` says which), `interleave` (a point the platform waits at, `published`, `claimed`, `removed`, `opened`, `entering`, `distributing`, `distributed` or `delivering`), `freeSpace`, `diskFull`, `contentPath(filesDirectory, hash)` (where the store keeps a content) and `test.contextOf(browser)` (the context the door seals for that browser's session), restoring at an instant of the test clock and, only here, with cut points; its tests run the real restic, and a global setup fails the run with `restic is required: brew install restic` without it; for the live channel `openChannel(browser)` and `readEvents(stream)`, a reader of its events and comments whatever way the bytes split, and option `channelQueue`, a lower limit of a channel's queue; for notices option `push`, a fake push service (`fakePushService()`: the `hosts` the platform admits, which a test may change, every request it kept, and `answer`, with `neverAnswers`, a request pending until its signal aborts), `pushClient(name)`, a subscription on it with real keys whose `read` decrypts a request, and `vapidClaims(request, key)`, which checks its signature, and option `origins` (`TEST_ORIGIN` and `TEST_PUSH_SUBJECT`, the subject of VAPID, unless given); without `push` a delivery that would send fails; later the fake model and fake channel. Only tests import it.
- `api-client`: `clientFor(contract, transport)`, a thin oRPC link. It holds no contract: callers pass theirs. A write's idempotency key and a sensitive operation's step-up proof go in the call's context and travel as headers; authentication and retries belong to the caller.

`utils`, `i18n`, `ui` and `api-client` know nothing of platform, its contracts included.
